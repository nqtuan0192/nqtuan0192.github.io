About me

I am a lecturer in computer science at Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST).

My research focuses on artificial intelligence, knowledge representation & reasoning, and optimization. My interests include logic programming, answer set programming, and linear algebraic approaches to logical reasoning.

I earned an engineering degree in Computer Science in 2015 and a master’s degree in 2019, both at HUST. In 2022, I completed my Ph.D. in Informatics at SOKENDAI (The Graduate University for Advanced Studies), Japan, with doctoral research at the National Institute of Informatics (NII).

My background also includes work as a project researcher in Katsumi Inoue’s lab, in NII’s Principles of Informatics Research Division, Tokyo. My doctoral studies were supported by MEXT and JICA Innovative Asia scholarships.
